# Third-party licensing

LocalRedact does not vendor third-party code in its wheel. Installation resolves
dependencies from their respective distributions, each governed by its own
license.

## PyMuPDF and MuPDF

PyMuPDF is a mandatory runtime dependency. Its maintainers offer PyMuPDF and
MuPDF under open-source AGPL terms and commercial licenses. LocalRedact therefore
uses AGPL-3.0-only for its own source distribution.

- PyMuPDF licensing: <https://pymupdf.readthedocs.io/en/latest/about.html#license-and-copyright>
- Artifex licensing options: <https://artifex.com/licensing/>

Organizations that cannot meet applicable AGPL requirements should obtain legal
advice and appropriate commercial licensing before distributing or operating the
combined software. This notice is informational and is not legal advice.
